## GENERAL INSTRUCTIONS

<!-- SENTINEL: GENERAL_INSTRUCTION_A -->

### A. Rule as to Use of Form S-4

- **1.** This Form may be used for registration under the Securities Act of 1933 ("Securities Act") of securities to be issued:
  - **(1)** In a transaction of the type specified in paragraph (a) of Rule 145 (§230.145 of this chapter);
  - **(2)** In a merger in which the applicable state law would not require the solicitation of the votes or consents of all of the security holders of the company being acquired;
  - **(3)** In an exchange offer for securities of the issuer or another entity;
  - **(4)** In a public reoffering or resale of any such securities acquired pursuant to this registration statement; or
  - **(5)** In more than one of the kinds of transaction listed in (1) through (4) registered on one registration statement.
- **2. 20-Business-Day Prospectus Delivery:** If the registrant meets the requirements of and elects to comply with the provisions in any item of this Form or Form F-4 that provides for incorporation by reference of information about the registrant or the company being acquired, the prospectus must be sent to the security holders no later than 20 business days prior to the date on which the meeting of such security holders is held.
- **3.** This Form shall not be used if the registrant is a registered investment company or a business development company as defined in Section 2(a)(48) of the Investment Company Act of 1940.

## PART I—INFORMATION REQUIRED IN THE PROSPECTUS

### A. INFORMATION ABOUT THE TRANSACTION

- **Item 1. Forepart of Registration Statement and Outside Front Cover Page of Prospectus:** Furnish Item 501 of Regulation S-K.
- **Item 2. Inside Front and Outside Back Cover Pages of Prospectus:** State that prospectus incorporates important information by reference and is available upon request within 5 business days.
- **Item 3. Risk Factors and Other Information:** Summarize the transaction, businesses, comparative market values, voting shares held by insiders, regulatory approvals, and appraisal rights.
- **Item 4. Terms of the Transaction:** Summarize acquisition agreement terms, reasons for transaction, securities description, differences in security holder rights, and federal income tax consequences.
- **Item 5. Pro Forma Financial Information:** Provide Article 11 Regulation S-X pro forma financial statements for the transaction.
- **Item 6. Material Contracts with the Company Being Acquired:** Detail material contracts or transactions during the past two years.
- **Item 7. Additional Information Required for Reoffering by Persons Deemed to Be Underwriters:** Selling security holder disclosures under Item 507 of Regulation S-K.
- **Item 8. Interests of Named Experts and Counsel:** Item 509 disclosures.
- **Item 9. Disclosure of Commission Position on Indemnification for Securities Act Liabilities:** Item 510 disclosures.

### B. INFORMATION ABOUT THE REGISTRANT

- **Item 10. Information with Respect to S-3 Registrants:** Description of business and incorporation by reference.
- **Item 11. Incorporation of Certain Information by Reference:** Form 10-K, 10-Q, 8-K incorporated documents.
- **Item 12. Information with Respect to S-3 Registrants (Alternative):** Full financial disclosures.
- **Item 14. Information with Respect to Registrants Other Than S-3 Registrants:** Complete Item 101-404 Regulation S-K disclosures.

### C. INFORMATION ABOUT THE COMPANY BEING ACQUIRED

- **Item 15–17.** Information corresponding to S-3 or non-S-3 standards for the target company.

### D. VOTING AND MANAGEMENT INFORMATION

- **Item 18. Information if Proxies, Consents or Authorizations are to be Solicited:** Proxy statement disclosures under Regulation 14A.
- **Item 19. Information if Proxies, Consents or Authorizations are not to be Solicited:** Information statement disclosures under Regulation 14C.
