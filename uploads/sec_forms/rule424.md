# RULE 424 PROSPECTUSES: PROSPECTUS FILINGS & SUPPLEMENT MATRIX

Filing framework and prospectus supplement standards pursuant to Rule 424 under the Securities Act of 1933.

<!-- SENTINEL: REGULATORY_FRAMEWORK -->
## REGULATORY FRAMEWORK

Every prospectus filed pursuant to Rule 424 must indicate on the upper right corner of the cover page the paragraph and subparagraph of Rule 424 under which the filing is made and the file number of the registration statement to which it relates.

**Securities Act Rule Reference:** 17 CFR § 230.424  
**Primary Registration Links:** Form S-1, Form S-3, Form S-4, Form S-11, Form F-1, Form F-3

### Filing Designation & Rule Paragraphs

*(Check the appropriate filing type)*

- [ ] **Rule 424(a):** Prospectus filed prior to the effective date of the registration statement (Preliminary Prospectus / "Red Herring"). Must bear the legend required by Item 501(b)(10) of Regulation S-K.
- [ ] **Rule 424(b)(1):** Prospectus disclosing pricing and terms information previously omitted from the registration statement in reliance on Rule 430A (§230.430A). Must be filed within two business days after determination of offering price or first use.
- [ ] **Rule 424(b)(2):** Primary offering of securities on a continuous or delayed basis under Rule 415(a)(1)(x) (e.g., debt shelf takedowns, medium-term notes, structured equity programs). Must be filed within two business days of pricing.
- [ ] **Rule 424(b)(3):** Prospectus reflecting substantive facts, material amendments, or events after the effective date of the registration statement that constitute additions or changes to previous disclosures.
- [ ] **Rule 424(b)(4):** Prospectus combining Rule 424(b)(1) pricing information and Rule 424(b)(3) post-effective substantive amendments.
- [ ] **Rule 424(b)(5):** Prospectus combining Rule 424(b)(2) shelf offering terms and Rule 424(b)(3) substantive event disclosures.
- [ ] **Rule 424(b)(7):** Prospectus filed in connection with resale offerings by selling security holders pursuant to Rule 415(a)(1)(i) under Form S-3 or F-3.
- [ ] **Rule 424(b)(8):** Prospectus supplement filed in connection with an exchange offer or acquisition under Rule 415(a)(1)(viii).

## STANDARD PROSPECTUS SUPPLEMENT STRUCTURE

<!-- SENTINEL: SECTION_1 -->
### 1. Cover Page & Preliminary Legend
- Mandatory legend under Item 501(b)(10) of Regulation S-K if preliminary:  
  > *"The information in this preliminary prospectus supplement is not complete and may be changed. We may not sell these securities until the registration statement filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission is effective..."*
- Ticker symbol, principal trading exchange, and last reported sale price.

<!-- SENTINEL: SECTION_2 -->
### 2. Prospectus Summary & Terms of the Offering
- Overview of the business, competitive advantages, and growth strategies.
- Offering summary: securities offered, maturity date, interest rate / coupon, redemption provisions, conversion rights, and ranking.

<!-- SENTINEL: SECTION_3 -->
### 3. Risk Factors
- Specific risks related to the offering, structural subordination, dilution, interest rate volatility, and regulatory developments.

<!-- SENTINEL: SECTION_4 -->
### 4. Use of Proceeds
- Itemized allocation of net proceeds (debt repayment, capital expenditures, strategic acquisitions, working capital).

<!-- SENTINEL: SECTION_5 -->
### 5. Capitalization & Dilution
- Pro forma balance sheet capitalization reflecting the offering and use of proceeds.

<!-- SENTINEL: SECTION_6 -->
### 6. Underwriting / Plan of Distribution
- Underwriting agreement commitments, overallotment (greenshoe) option, stabilization activities, lock-up agreements (standard 90 or 180 days).

<!-- SENTINEL: SECTION_7 -->
### 7. Validity of Securities & Experts
- Legal opinion on the validity of the securities (Item 601 Exhibit 5) and independent auditor consents (Exhibit 23).
