## SCHEDULE 14A ITEMS

<!-- SENTINEL: ITEM_1 -->

### Item 1. Date, Time and Place Information

- **(a)** State the date, time and place of the meeting of security holders, and the complete mailing address, including ZIP Code, of the principal executive offices of the registrant, unless such information is otherwise provided in materials accompanying the proxy statement.
- **(b)** On the first page of the proxy statement, state the approximate date on which the proxy statement and form of proxy are first sent or given to security holders.
- **(c)** Furnish the information required to be in the proxy statement by Rule 14a-5(e) (§240.14a-5(e) of this chapter).

<!-- SENTINEL: ITEM_2 -->

### Item 2. Revocability of Proxy

State whether or not the person giving the proxy has the power to revoke it. If the right of revocation before the proxy is exercised is subject to conditions or limitations, outline them briefly.

<!-- SENTINEL: ITEM_3 -->

### Item 3. Dissenters' Right of Appraisal

Outline briefly the rights of appraisal or similar rights of dissenters with respect to any matter to be acted upon and indicate any statutory procedure required to be followed by dissenting security holders to perfect such rights.

<!-- SENTINEL: ITEM_4 -->

### Item 4. Persons Making the Solicitation

- **(a) Solicitations not subject to Rule 14a-12(c):**
  - **(1)** If the solicitation is made by the registrant, so state. Give the name of any director of the registrant who has informed the registrant in writing that he intends to oppose any action intended to be taken by the registrant and indicate the action which he intends to oppose.
  - **(2)** If the solicitation is made otherwise than by the registrant, so state and give the names of the participants in the solicitation, as defined in paragraphs (a)(iii), (iv), (v) and (vi) of Instruction 3 to this Item.
  - **(3)** If the solicitation is to be made otherwise than by the use of the mails or electronically, describe the methods to be employed. If the solicitation is to be made by specially engaged employees or paid solicitors, state (i) the material features of any contract or arrangement, (ii) the cost thereof, and (iii) the names of the persons who will make the solicitation.
  - **(4)** State the names of the persons by whom the cost of solicitation has been or will be borne, directly or indirectly.
- **(b) Solicitations subject to Rule 14a-12(c):** State by whom the solicitation is made and the nature of the interests of the participants.

<!-- SENTINEL: ITEM_5 -->

### Item 5. Interest of Certain Persons in Matters to Be Acted Upon

Describe any substantial interest, direct or indirect, by contracts or otherwise, of each of the following persons in any matter to be acted upon, other than elections to office:

- **(a)** If the solicitation is made on behalf of the registrant, each person who has been a director or executive officer of the registrant at any time since the beginning of the last fiscal year.
- **(b)** If the solicitation is made otherwise than on behalf of the registrant, each participant in the solicitation.
- **(c)** Each nominee for election as a director of the registrant.
- **(d)** Each associate of any of the foregoing persons.

<!-- SENTINEL: ITEM_6 -->

### Item 6. Voting Securities and Principal Holders Thereof

- **(a)** State the number of shares outstanding of each class of voting securities entitled to vote at the meeting, and the number of votes to which each class is entitled.
- **(b)** Give the record date with respect to the solicitation.
- **(c)** If action is to be taken with respect to the election of directors and cumulative voting rights exist, provide the disclosure required by Item 403 of Regulation S-K.
- **(d)** Furnish the information required by Item 403 of Regulation S-K (§229.403 of this chapter) with respect to beneficial ownership of certain beneficial owners and management.

<!-- SENTINEL: ITEM_7 -->

### Item 7. Directors and Executive Officers

If action is to be taken with respect to the election of directors, furnish the following information:

- **(a)** Information required by Instruction 4 to Item 103 of Regulation S-K (§229.103 of this chapter) with respect to legal proceedings.
- **(b)** Information required by Items 401, 404(a) and (b), 405, 407(a), (b), (c)(1), (c)(2), (d)(1), (d)(2), (d)(3), (d)(4), (d)(5), (e)(1), (e)(2), (e)(3), (f) and (g) of Regulation S-K (§229.401, §229.404, §229.405, and §229.407 of this chapter).

<!-- SENTINEL: ITEM_8 -->

### Item 8. Compensation of Directors and Executive Officers

Furnish the information required by Item 402 of Regulation S-K (§229.402 of this chapter) and paragraphs (e)(4) and (e)(5) of Item 407 of Regulation S-K (§229.407 of this chapter) if action is to be taken with regard to:

- **(a)** The election of directors;
- **(b)** Any bonus, profit sharing or other compensation plan in which any director, nominee for election, or executive officer will participate;
- **(c)** Any pension or retirement plan in which any such person will participate; or
- **(d)** The granting or extension to any such person of any options, warrants or rights to purchase any securities.

### Item 9. Independent Public Accountants

If the solicitation is made on behalf of the registrant and relates to (1) the annual (or special in lieu of annual) meeting of security holders at which directors are to be elected, or (2) the election, approval or ratification of the registrant's accountant, furnish the information required by Item 304(a) of Regulation S-K (§229.304(a) of this chapter) and disclose:

- **(a)** The name of the principal accountant selected or being recommended to shareholders;
- **(b)** The name of the principal accountant for the fiscal year most recently completed if different; and
- **(c)** Audit Fees, Audit-Related Fees, Tax Fees, and All Other Fees billed for each of the last two fiscal years.

### Item 10. Compensation Plans

If action is to be taken with respect to any plan pursuant to which cash or noncash compensation may be paid or distributed, furnish the information required by Item 10, including equity compensation plan tables under Item 201(d) of Regulation S-K.

<!-- SENTINEL: ITEM_14 -->

### Item 14. Mergers, Consolidations, Acquisitions and Similar Matters

If action is to be taken with respect to any transaction involving (1) a merger or consolidation, (2) the acquisition of another entity, (3) the acquisition of a business, or (4) the sale or other transfer of all or any substantial part of assets, provide detailed transaction summaries, accounting treatment, regulatory approvals, and pro forma financial statements pursuant to Article 11 of Regulation S-X.

<!-- SENTINEL: ITEM_21 -->

### Item 21. Voting Procedures

As to each matter which is to be submitted to a vote of security holders, furnish the following information:

- **(a)** State the vote required for approval or election, other than for the approval of auditors.
- **(b)** Disclose the method by which votes will be counted, including the treatment and effect of abstentions and broker non-votes under applicable state law and registrant charter/bylaws.

<!-- SENTINEL: ITEM_24 -->

### Item 24. Shareholder Proposals

If any shareholder proposal is submitted pursuant to Rule 14a-8, include the proposal and supporting statement, together with the Board's statement of opposition and recommendation.

<!-- SENTINEL: ITEM_25 -->

### Item 25. Calculation of Filing Fee Tables

Furnish the information required by Item 25, in substantially the tabular form indicated in Item 16(c) of Form S-1, if a fee is required to be paid under Exchange Act Rule 0-11.
