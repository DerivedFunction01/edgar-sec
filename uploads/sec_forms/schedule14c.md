## SCHEDULE 14C ITEMS

<!-- SENTINEL: ITEM_1 -->
### Item 1. Information Required by Items of Schedule 14A
Furnish the information called for by all of the items of Schedule 14A of Regulation 14A (other than Items 1(c), 2, 4 and 5) which would be applicable to any matter to be acted upon at the meeting or by written consent if proxies were to be solicited in connection with the matter.
- **Matter Acted Upon:** Detailed description of corporate action (e.g., charter amendments, share authorization increases, reverse stock splits, corporate name change, adoption of equity plans).
- **Consenting Stockholders:** Number of votes and percentage of voting power represented by majority stockholders who executed the written consent.
- **Effective Date:** Disclose that pursuant to Rule 14c-2, corporate action taken by written consent will not take effect until 20 calendar days after the mailing of this definitive information statement.

<!-- SENTINEL: ITEM_2 -->
### Item 2. Statement That Proxies Are Not Solicited
State the boldfaced mandatory legend on the first page of the Information Statement:
> *"WE ARE NOT ASKING YOU FOR A PROXY AND YOU ARE REQUESTED NOT TO SEND US A PROXY."*

<!-- SENTINEL: ITEM_3 -->
### Item 3. Interest of Certain Persons in or Opposition to Matters to Be Acted Upon
- **(a)** Describe briefly any substantial interest, direct or indirect, by security holdings or otherwise, of each person who has been a director or executive officer of the registrant at any time since the beginning of the last fiscal year.
- **(b)** Give the name of any director who has informed the registrant in writing that he or she intends to oppose any action to be taken by the registrant and indicate the action which he or she intends to oppose.

<!-- SENTINEL: ITEM_4 -->
### Item 4. Proposals by Security Holders
Furnish the information required by Rule 14a-8 with respect to any proposal submitted by a security holder that is omitted from the information statement.

<!-- SENTINEL: ITEM_5 -->
### Item 5. Delivery of Documents to Security Holders Sharing an Address
State whether only one information statement is being delivered to multiple security holders sharing an address (Householding disclosure under Rule 14c-3(c)).
