## SECTION 1 - CONFLICT MINERALS DISCLOSURE

<!-- SENTINEL: ITEM_1_01 -->
### Item 1.01 Conflict Minerals Disclosure and Report

- **(a) Scope & Applicability:** Applies to registrants that manufacture or contract to manufacture products for which conflict minerals (tantalum, tin, tungsten, or gold / "3TG") are necessary to the functionality or production of a product.
- **(b) Reasonable Country of Origin Inquiry (RCOI):**
  - Registrant must conduct a good-faith inquiry reasonably designed to determine whether any of the conflict minerals originated in the Democratic Republic of the Congo or an adjoining country (Covered Countries).
  - If RCOI determined conflict minerals did not originate in Covered Countries or came from recycled/scrap sources, disclose this determination and describe the RCOI process in the body of Form SD.
- **(c) Conflict Minerals Report Requirement:**
  - If registrant has reason to believe conflict minerals originated in Covered Countries and did not come from recycled/scrap sources, registrant must exercise due diligence conforming to a nationally or internationally recognized framework (e.g., OECD Due Diligence Guidance).
  - File a Conflict Minerals Report as Exhibit 1.01 to this Form SD.
- **(d) Internet Availability:** Disclose the publicly available Internet website where the Form SD and Conflict Minerals Report are posted.

## SECTION 2 - RESOURCE EXTRACTION PAYMENTS REPORT

<!-- SENTINEL: ITEM_2_01 -->
### Item 2.01 Resource Extraction Payments Report

- **(a) Disclosure Requirements:** Requires resource extraction issuers engaged in commercial development of oil, natural gas, or minerals to disclose payments made to the U.S. federal government or foreign governments (including subnational governments).
- **(b) Submission Format:** Furnish information in interactive data format using the XBRL specialized technical taxonomy (Exhibit 2.01).
- **(c) Payment Categories:** Disclose taxes, royalties, fees (including license fees), production entitlements, bonuses, dividends, and infrastructure improvements where project payment aggregate equals or exceeds $100,000.
