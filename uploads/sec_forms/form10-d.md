## PART I - DISTRIBUTION INFORMATION

<!-- SENTINEL: ITEM_1 -->
### Item 1. Distribution and Pool Performance Information

Furnish the information required by Item 1121 of Regulation AB (§229.1121 of this chapter). Attach the distribution report delivered to the trustee or certificateholders as Exhibit 99.1.
- Aggregate pool asset balances, principal/interest collections, and charge-offs
- Pass-through distribution amounts per class / tranche (principal, interest, carryforward amounts)
- Credit enhancement draws, reserve account balances, and liquidity facility status
- Delinquency statistics (30-59 days, 60-89 days, 90+ days delinquent) and loss severities

<!-- SENTINEL: ITEM_2 -->
### Item 2. Legal Proceedings

Furnish the information required by Item 1117 of Regulation AB (§229.1117 of this chapter) regarding material pending legal proceedings against the sponsor, depositor, trustee, issuing entity, or 20% loan servicer.

<!-- SENTINEL: ITEM_3 -->
### Item 3. Sale of Securities and Use of Proceeds

Furnish the information required by Item 2 of Part II of Form 10-Q and Item 701 of Regulation S-K regarding unregistered sales of asset-backed securities.

<!-- SENTINEL: ITEM_4 -->
### Item 4. Defaults Upon Senior Securities

Furnish the information required by Item 3 of Part II of Form 10-Q regarding any material default in the payment of principal, interest, or sinking fund installments.

<!-- SENTINEL: ITEM_5 -->
### Item 5. Submission of Matters to a Vote of Security Holders

Furnish the information required by Item 4 of Part II of Form 10-Q regarding any matter submitted to a vote of pool certificateholders or noteholders.

<!-- SENTINEL: ITEM_6 -->
### Item 6. Significant Obligors of Pool Assets

Provide updated disclosure under Item 1112(b) of Regulation AB for any obligor representing 10% or more of pool assets (or 20% or more requiring audited financial statements).

<!-- SENTINEL: ITEM_7 -->
### Item 7. Significant Enhancement Provider Information

Furnish information required by Item 1114(b)(2) (derivative counterparty) or Item 1115(b) (credit enhancement provider) if financial condition thresholds are crossed.

<!-- SENTINEL: ITEM_8 -->
### Item 8. Other Information

Disclose any information required to be reported on Form 8-K during the distribution period that was not previously reported.

<!-- SENTINEL: ITEM_9 -->
### Item 9. Asset-Backed Issuer Distribution Report Exhibit Information

Disclose the filing of any asset-level data file (Exhibit 102) and asset-level compliance table (Exhibit 103) required under Rule 13a-17.
