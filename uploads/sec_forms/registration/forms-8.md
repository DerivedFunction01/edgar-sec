## GENERAL INSTRUCTIONS

<!-- SENTINEL: GENERAL_INSTRUCTION_A -->

### A. Rule as to Use of Form S-8

- **1. Eligible Registrants & Securities:** Any registrant that is subject to the reporting requirements of Section 13 or 15(d) of the Exchange Act, has filed all required reports for the preceding 12 months, and is not a shell company may use this Form to register:
  - **(a)** Securities of the registrant to be offered under any employee benefit plan to its employees or employees of its subsidiaries or parents; and
  - **(b)** Interests in such plans, if such interests constitute separate securities.
- **2. Employee Definition:** Includes employees, directors, officers, consultants, advisors (natural persons providing bona fide non-capital raising services), and immediate family members acquiring options via gift or domestic relations order.

<!-- SENTINEL: GENERAL_INSTRUCTION_C -->

### C. Reoffers and Resales

Securities acquired by affiliates under a Form S-8 registration statement ("control securities") or restricted securities issued under an employee benefit plan may be reoffered or resold on a continuous or delayed basis pursuant to Rule 415 by means of a separate reoffer prospectus prepared in accordance with Form S-3.

<!-- SENTINEL: GENERAL_INSTRUCTION_D -->

### D. Immediate Effectiveness

A registration statement on Form S-8 and any post-effective amendments become effective immediately upon filing pursuant to Rule 462 and Rule 464. Delaying amendments are not permitted.

## PART I—INFORMATION REQUIRED IN THE SECTION 10(a) PROSPECTUS

> **Note:** The documents containing the information specified in Part I are sent or given to employees pursuant to Rule 428(b)(1) and are not required to be filed with the Commission.

- **Item 1. Plan Information:** Deliver material information regarding plan operations, eligibility, contribution percentages, tax consequences, ERISA applicability, withdrawal conditions, and investment alternatives.
- **Item 2. Registrant Information and Employee Plan Annual Information:** Statement advising participants of the availability without charge, upon oral or written request, of documents incorporated by reference.

<!-- SENTINEL: PART_II -->

## PART II—INFORMATION REQUIRED IN THE REGISTRATION STATEMENT

- **Item 3. Incorporation of Documents by Reference:** Incorporates by reference (a) the registrant's latest annual report on Form 10-K, (b) all subsequent Exchange Act reports filed prior to filing, and (c) description of securities registered under Section 12.
- **Item 4. Description of Securities:** Required if securities are not registered under Section 12 of the Exchange Act.
- **Item 5. Interests of Named Experts and Counsel:** Disclose interests under Item 509 of Regulation S-K.
- **Item 6. Indemnification of Directors and Officers:** Disclose Item 702 of Regulation S-K indemnification provisions.
- **Item 7. Exemption from Registration Claimed:** Disclose basis of exemption for restricted securities being registered for resale.
- **Item 8. Exhibits:** Furnish Item 601 exhibits (including plan documents and legal opinions).
- **Item 9. Undertakings:** Include undertakings required by Item 512 of Regulation S-K.
