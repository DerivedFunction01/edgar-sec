## INFORMATION REQUIRED IN REGISTRATION STATEMENT

<!-- SENTINEL: ITEM_1 -->
### Item 1. Business
Furnish the information required by Item 101 of Regulation S-K (general development of business, products/services, supply chain, intellectual property, human capital).

<!-- SENTINEL: ITEM_1A -->
### Item 1A. Risk Factors
Furnish the information required by Item 105 of Regulation S-K.

<!-- SENTINEL: ITEM_2 -->
### Item 2. Financial Information
Furnish the information required by Items 301, 303 (Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations - MD&A), and 305 (Quantitative and Qualitative Disclosures About Market Risk) of Regulation S-K.

<!-- SENTINEL: ITEM_3 -->
### Item 3. Properties
Furnish the information required by Item 102 of Regulation S-K.

<!-- SENTINEL: ITEM_4 -->
### Item 4. Security Ownership of Certain Beneficial Owners and Management
Furnish the information required by Item 403 of Regulation S-K (5% holders, directors, named executive officers).

<!-- SENTINEL: ITEM_5 -->
### Item 5. Directors and Executive Officers
Furnish the information required by Item 401 of Regulation S-K.

<!-- SENTINEL: ITEM_6 -->
### Item 6. Executive Compensation
Furnish the information required by Item 402 of Regulation S-K (Compensation Discussion and Analysis, Summary Compensation Table, Grants of Plan-Based Awards).

<!-- SENTINEL: ITEM_7 -->
### Item 7. Certain Relationships and Related Transactions, and Director Independence
Furnish the information required by Item 404 and Item 407(a) of Regulation S-K.

<!-- SENTINEL: ITEM_8 -->
### Item 8. Legal Proceedings
Furnish the information required by Item 103 of Regulation S-K.

<!-- SENTINEL: ITEM_9 -->
### Item 9. Market Price of and Dividends on the Registrant's Common Equity and Related Stockholder Matters
Furnish the information required by Item 201 of Regulation S-K.

<!-- SENTINEL: ITEM_10 -->
### Item 10. Recent Sales of Unregistered Securities
Furnish the information required by Item 701 of Regulation S-K for all securities sold within the past three years.

<!-- SENTINEL: ITEM_11 -->
### Item 11. Description of Registrant's Securities to be Registered
Furnish the information required by Item 202 of Regulation S-K.

<!-- SENTINEL: ITEM_12 -->
### Item 12. Indemnification of Directors and Officers
Furnish the information required by Item 702 of Regulation S-K.

<!-- SENTINEL: ITEM_13 -->
### Item 13. Financial Statements and Supplementary Data
Furnish all financial statements required by Regulation S-X and supplementary data required by Item 302 of Regulation S-K.

<!-- SENTINEL: ITEM_14 -->
### Item 14. Changes in and Disagreements with Accountants on Accounting and Financial Disclosure
Furnish the information required by Item 304 of Regulation S-K.

<!-- SENTINEL: ITEM_15 -->
### Item 15. Financial Statements and Exhibits
- **(a)** List separately all financial statements filed as part of the registration statement.
- **(b)** Furnish the exhibits required by Item 601 of Regulation S-K.
