## SCHEDULE TO DISCLOSURE ITEMS (REGULATION M-A)

<!-- SENTINEL: ITEM_1 -->
### Item 1. Summary Term Sheet
Furnish the information required by Item 1001 of Regulation M-A (§229.1001). Provide a bulleted summary of the core transaction terms, offer price, expiration date, proration mechanisms, conditions, and financing.

<!-- SENTINEL: ITEM_2 -->
### Item 2. Subject Company Information
Furnish the information required by Item 1002 of Regulation M-A (§229.1002):
- Name, principal executive offices, and telephone number of subject company.
- Title and number of shares outstanding of each class of equity securities subject to the offer.
- High and low sales prices for each quarterly period during the past two years.

<!-- SENTINEL: ITEM_3 -->
### Item 3. Identity and Background of Filing Person
Furnish the information required by Item 1003 of Regulation M-A (§229.1003) for bidder/offeror persons, controlling entities, executive officers, and directors (criminal history, citizenship, business address, past 5 years employment).

<!-- SENTINEL: ITEM_4 -->
### Item 4. Terms of the Transaction
Furnish the information required by Item 1004 of Regulation M-A (§229.1004):
- Total number of securities sought, consideration offered per unit, expiration date, and withdrawal rights procedures.
- Tax consequences to security holders, accounting treatment, and proration terms.

<!-- SENTINEL: ITEM_5 -->
### Item 5. Past Contacts, Transactions, Negotiations and Agreements
Furnish the information required by Item 1005 of Regulation M-A (§229.1005) between bidder and subject company, including merger talks, confidentiality agreements, and commercial transactions.

<!-- SENTINEL: ITEM_6 -->
### Item 6. Purposes of the Transaction and Plans or Proposals
Furnish the information required by Item 1006 of Regulation M-A (§229.1006):
- Extraordinary corporate transactions (mergers, reorganizations, asset sales, board changes).
- Plans that would cause delisting from an exchange or termination of Section 12(g) registration.

<!-- SENTINEL: ITEM_7 -->
### Item 7. Source and Amount of Funds or Other Consideration
Furnish the information required by Item 1007 of Regulation M-A (§229.1007):
- Total amount of funds required to purchase securities and pay related fees and expenses.
- Material terms of all credit agreements, term loans, or bridge financing commitments.

<!-- SENTINEL: ITEM_8 -->
### Item 8. Interest in Securities of the Subject Company
Furnish the information required by Item 1008 of Regulation M-A (§229.1008):
- Aggregate number and percentage of subject securities beneficially owned by filing persons and affiliates.
- Itemized summary of transactions in subject securities within the past 60 days.

<!-- SENTINEL: ITEM_9 -->
### Item 9. Persons/Assets Retained, Employed, Compensated or Used
Furnish the information required by Item 1009 of Regulation M-A (§229.1009) regarding dealer-managers, information agents, and depositaries.

<!-- SENTINEL: ITEM_10 -->
### Item 10. Financial Statements
Furnish the information required by Item 1010 of Regulation M-A (§229.1010) if bidder financial statements are material to the investor's decision.

<!-- SENTINEL: ITEM_11 -->
### Item 11. Additional Information
Furnish the information required by Item 1011 of Regulation M-A (§229.1011): regulatory approvals (HSR antitrust, CFIUS), margin requirements, and material pending litigation.

<!-- SENTINEL: ITEM_12 -->
### Item 12. Exhibits
Furnish the exhibits required by Item 1016 of Regulation M-A (§229.1016):
- **Exhibit (a):** Offer to Purchase, Letter of Transmittal, Notice of Guaranteed Delivery, Press Releases.
- **Exhibit (b):** Loan agreements and debt commitments.
- **Exhibit (d):** Contracts, agreements, and confidentiality letters.
- **Exhibit (g):** Solicitations or recommendations.

<!-- SENTINEL: ITEM_13 -->
### Item 13. Information Required by Schedule 13E-3
If filed as a joint Schedule 13E-3 transaction, furnish all information required by Item 7 (Purposes, Alternatives, Reasons), Item 8 (Fairness of the Transaction), and Item 9 (Reports, Opinions, Appraisals).
