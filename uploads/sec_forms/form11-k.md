## REQUIRED INFORMATION

Financial statements, schedules, and exhibits prepared in accordance with the financial reporting requirements of the Employee Retirement Income Security Act of 1974 ("ERISA") or Regulation S-X.

<!-- SENTINEL: ITEM_1 -->
### Item 1. Itemized Financial Statements & Accounting Standards

- **(a) ERISA Alternative:** In lieu of the requirements of Items 1, 2, and 3 below, plan administrators may file plan financial statements and schedules prepared in accordance with the financial reporting requirements of ERISA. Such financial statements must be examined by an independent certified public accountant in accordance with generally accepted auditing standards.
- **(b) Regulation S-X Requirements:** If not electing the ERISA alternative, the financial statements required by Regulation S-X (Rule 6A-01 through 6A-05) must be provided.

<!-- SENTINEL: ITEM_2 -->
### Item 2. Statements of Financial Condition

Provide statements of financial condition of the plan as of the end of the latest two fiscal years of the plan, compiled in accordance with Rule 6A-03 of Regulation S-X, reflecting:
- Cash and cash equivalents
- Investments at fair value (itemized by category: common stock, mutual funds, collective trusts, stable value funds, and participant loans)
- Receivables (employer contributions, participant contributions, interest and dividends)
- Liabilities and total plan net assets available for benefits

<!-- SENTINEL: ITEM_3 -->
### Item 3. Statements of Income and Changes in Plan Equity

Provide statements of income and changes in plan equity of the plan for each of the latest three fiscal years of the plan (or two years under the ERISA option), showing:
- Net appreciation/depreciation in fair value of investments
- Investment income (dividends, interest)
- Contributions (employer contributions, employee contributions, rollovers)
- Benefits paid directly to participants and administrative expenses
- Net increase/decrease in net assets for the period

<!-- SENTINEL: ITEM_4 -->
### Item 4. Exhibits
