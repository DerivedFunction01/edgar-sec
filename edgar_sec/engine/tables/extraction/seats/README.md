# seats

## Purpose

Seats and gates: the genre seat, section gate, TOC gate, and primary gate
that refuse or seat geometries before and during claiming, and the family
seats (statement, commitments, deferred tax) that claim from a geometry's
own carried context.

## Contracts

- **Document furniture is refused at the genre seat, upstream of all
  claiming**: contents and index pages, closing-span and exhibit-index
  tables, empty bodies, folios, and unseated zero-numeric fragments never
  reach the cascade as per-family imposters.
- **Section position is a genre gate, not evidence**: a statement-family
  claim seated in an MD&A or directors section is refused by position alone,
  with the refusal recorded in the evidence trail; location never scores.
- **A seat never re-derives a filing-level fact**: the TOC span, closing
  span, region map, and exhibit inventory ride the context produced by
  `assembly.context_production`; a seat re-deriving one is a defect.
- **Commitments claims sit in the geometry-text seat**: the seat reads the
  geometry's own text and claims only where the carried context seats the
  geometry in the MD&A seat or a note region.

## Deliberate gaps

- **No scoring lives here**: seats refuse or claim on carried facts; the
  scored competition is `cascade/`.
