# form

## Purpose

The cascade's form layer: evidence that determines what kind of table the
cascade is looking at — the admission core-signal reads, the core-form
evaluator, the claim-level form-separation determination, the canonical
input classes over the grid, and the labor-form signature seat.

## Contracts

- **Form evidence classifies the input, not a family's substance**:
  statement-family evidence lives in `balance_sheet/`, `income_statement/`,
  and `equity/`; this layer reads form and class only.
- **Evidence scores, it does not refuse**: a failed signal records a
  zero-point signal, never a gate; refusal lives in `seats/` and `vetoes/`.

## Deliberate gaps

- **The form tests are not here**: the evaluators that compose this layer's
  determinations live in `form_tests/`, over the specs in `form_specs/`.
