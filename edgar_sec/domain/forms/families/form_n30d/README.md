# form_n30d

Domain models for Form N-30D, the shareholder reports registered
management investment companies filed, its N-30D/A amendments, and
the N-30B-2 reports transmitted under the same regime: the family's
processing profile and section taxonomy over the shared text and
table-geometry machinery. The section vocabulary is the one filed
specimens evidence, established by a specimen census of the line's
filing population (92,818 catalog filings, 1994–2026) after the
line's catalog record proved 54% silent on primary documents: the
silent share's bodies are full shareholder reports reached through
the filings' full-submission text, carrying the same statement
vocabulary as the profiled N-CSR family plus the letter and
performance sections of the earlier regime.

## Contracts

- **Text path**: the profile offers RAW and RENDER_TO_TXT with text
  preferred; N-30D/A and N-30B-2 resolve to the same catalog entry
  through the family alias.
- **Section resolution**: with the profile's sections flag set,
  normalize and the shared analysis path resolve sections from the
  catalog entry's taxonomy items, in filed order.
- **Report projection**: filings of the family project as
  FormN30DReport.

## Deliberate gaps

- **No XBRL leg**: no specimen in the census carries an XBRL
  instance, so the profile claims no XBRL support.
- **No bespoke review analyzer**: the shared analysis path reads
  the catalog entry directly.
- **No notes-sequence flag**: notes detection was not measured for
  this family; the notes block resolves as a taxonomy section.
- **Silent-era absence at the catalog seam**: pre-2001 catalog
  records name no primary document; the census recovered those
  bodies through the full-submission text, but the catalog-driven
  acquisition path serves their absence until it learns that route.
