# form_4024b2

Domain models for Form 40-24B2, the filing of copies of sales
literature under section 24(b) of the Investment Company Act of 1940
and Rule 24b-2 thereunder, and its 40-24B2/A amendments: the family's
identity and processing profile only. The rule prescribes no form,
items, or section structure for the material filed — the filing is
the sales literature itself — so the family carries no taxonomy.

## Contracts

- **Text path**: the profile offers RAW and RENDER_TO_TXT with text
  preferred; 40-24B2/A resolves to the same catalog entry through
  the family alias.
- **Honest absence**: with no sections flag and no taxonomy items,
  normalize serves the body text and no section resolution; nothing
  is manufactured from marketing headings.

## Deliberate gaps

- **No section taxonomy**: Rule 24b-2 names no items or structure
  to model, so none is invented.
- **No cover profile**: no face-sheet vocabulary is claimed for
  sales literature; the generic cover profile serves the family.
- **No report projection**: the filing is the document itself; no
  family report shape exists.
